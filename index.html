This Week's Challenge

Make Your Own Blot

You saw the inkblots today. Now make one. Then tell me what you see in it.

What you need

One piece of paper. A pen, marker, or anything wet. Ink, paint, even a wet marker tip. A pen alone works fine.

What to do

  • 1Fold your paper in half.
  • 2Put some marks on one side. Anything. Do not plan it.
  • 3Fold it closed and press hard. Open it.
  • 4Look at what you made.
  • 5Write two sentences about what you see. Use the format below.

The format

"I see a ___. It is ___ and ___ and it looks like it is ___."
"It makes me think of ___ because ___."

Example

Name
Coach Shay
Class
1-1
Sentences
"I see a crow. It is dark and wide and it looks like it is about to land on something it should not touch."
"It makes me think of a secret because the wings look like they are hiding something underneath."

Write your name, your class number, and your two sentences on the paper with the blot. Leave it on Coach Shay's desk before you go.
